Overview

Introduction to FOCUS is a free entry-level course for people who consume FinOps reports or examine billing outputs without directly manipulating cost data. The FinOps Foundation says it covers FOCUS goals, sample use cases, and gathering FOCUS-formatted datasets. It offers organization-wide orientation for leadership, finance, procurement, product, engineering, allied personas, and people who build, market, or sell FinOps tools.

Syllabus & skills

  • FOCUS purpose and goals
  • Sample FOCUS use cases
  • Gathering FOCUS-formatted datasets
  • FOCUS orientation for FinOps report consumers

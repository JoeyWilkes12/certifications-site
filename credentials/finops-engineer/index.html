FinOps & cloud cost

Overview

FinOps Certified Engineer validates an engineering-focused grasp of cloud FinOps: making infrastructure decisions with cost and business value in view throughout the development lifecycle. The credential is aimed at engineers operating cloud infrastructure and covers practical disciplines such as efficiency, optimization, rightsizing, and tagging strategy. The Linux Foundation classifies it as an intermediate certification earned through study, activity-based learning, and a knowledge exam.

Syllabus & skills

  • Cloud cost optimization
  • Cloud efficiency
  • Cloud financial management
  • Cloud FinOps
  • Rightsizing
  • Tagging strategy
